*English · [中文](#chinese)*

The Ballon d'Or should be one of the most objective awards in football. Goals, assists, trophies, big-game performances: it is all on the record.

Yet every year it turns into a fan vote. "He's more talented." "He's more fun to watch." "His club is bigger." The loudest side tends to believe it holds the truth.

This year, Harry Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or.

This season he scored 73 goals in 65 games for club and country, helping Bayern Munich win the Bundesliga and the DFB-Pokal. At the World Cup he scored six goals and led England to the semi-finals. He is the first Englishman to win the award in 25 years, and the first winner from the Bundesliga since Matthias Sammer in 1996.

Even with a record like that, the debate rages on.

Some say he isn't "spectacular" enough. Some say he lacks star power. Others just "feel it should have been someone else." But a feeling is not an argument. Data doesn't care who you like. It only records what happened.

That is exactly why we look at data.

As a statistician, I deal with the same problem every day. Intuition is persuasive, stories are moving, reputations are loud, and all of them can mislead. Data may not be the whole truth, but it is something we can put on the table, test, and challenge. Opinions can differ; facts have to line up.

**In God we trust; all others must bring data.**

Next time you're about to argue for a conclusion, ask yourself one question first: where is my data?

Congratulations, Harry Kane. And here's to everyone who lets the evidence speak.

## 中文版 {#chinese}

金球奖本该是足坛最客观的奖项之一：进球、助攻、冠军、关键比赛，每一项都摆在那里，清清楚楚。

可每年颁奖前后，它都会变成一场粉丝的投票游戏。"他更有天赋""他踢得更好看""他的球队更有名"——声音最大的一方，往往觉得自己就是真相。

今年，哈里·凯恩拿到了 2026 年金球奖。

这个赛季，他为俱乐部和国家队出场 65 次、打进 73 球，帮助拜仁拿下德甲和德国杯；世界杯上打进 6 球，带领英格兰杀入四强。他是 25 年来第一位获得金球奖的英格兰球员，也是 1996 年萨默尔之后第一位来自德甲的得主。

就是这样一份成绩单，舆论里依然争议不断。

有人说他"不够惊艳"，有人说他"没有球星光环"，还有人说"总觉得应该是别人"。但"总觉得"不是论据。数据不在乎你喜欢谁，它只记录发生了什么。

这正是我们为什么要看数据。

作为统计人，我每天都在和同样的问题打交道：直觉很有说服力，故事很动人，名气很响亮——但它们都会骗人。数据未必是全部真相，可它是我们能拿上桌、能被检验、能被反驳的东西。观点可以分歧，事实需要对齐。

**In God we trust; all others must bring data.**

下一次，当你想为某个结论争论时，不妨先问自己一句：我的数据在哪里？

恭喜凯恩。也致敬每一个用事实说话的人。
