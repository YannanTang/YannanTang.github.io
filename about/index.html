About Me

Yannan Tang (汤岩楠), PhD in Statistics. I am a biostatistician with experience across early- and late-phase clinical trials in oncology, immunology, ophthalmology and metabolism. I enjoy combining scientific judgment and statistical programming with hands-on software development and community leadership.

Experience

Corcept Therapeutics, Associate Director, Biostatistics (2026 - present)
Primary statistician for early-phase oncology and metabolism studies, leading statistical design and analysis planning, including dose ranging, dose titration and SAP development, and owning CRO statistical delivery.

Genentech, Senior Biostatistician (2020 - 2026)
Developed statistical strategies for late-phase ophthalmology and immunology studies, and led statistical contributions to global regulatory filings, including a UK approval and an EMA submission. Mentored junior statisticians and interns.

Allergan (now part of AbbVie), R&D Biostatistics Intern (2019)
Applied propensity score methods and causal inference to post-market medical device data.

Community

Bay Area Biotech-Pharma Statistics Workshop (BBSW), VP of Technology (2024 - present; involved since 2022)
Created and lead the conference app, and lead a seven-person team across the app, website and video creation.

Expertise

Trial design and simulation, MMRM, time-to-event analysis, missing-data methods, causal inference, and regulatory statistics (SAPs, CSRs, IND/NDA/BLA/MAA, CDISC SDTM/ADaM). I program in R, SAS and Python.

Education

PhD in Statistics, University of California, Irvine, 2020
MS in Statistics, The George Washington University, 2015
BE in Civil Engineering, Tsinghua University, 2012

My doctoral work focused on statistical methods for individual-level geospatial data using generalized additive models, with applications in spatial epidemiology. With great honor, here I name my academic advisor, Professor Daniel Gillen and our collaborators, Professor Veronica Viera and Professor Scott Bartel.
